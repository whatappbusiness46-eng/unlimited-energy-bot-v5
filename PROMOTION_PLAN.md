# Member Growth Plan

## Best channels
1. Facebook groups/pages where earning bots are allowed.
2. TikTok/YouTube Shorts with a clear demo of the bot flow.
3. Telegram cross-promotion with relevant earning/community channels.
4. Referral rewards based on genuine activity, not fake signups.

## Content angle
Show the real flow: Join → complete verified task → earn points → withdraw according to the published rules.

Avoid fake screenshots, fake member counts, fake payment proofs, guaranteed-income claims, or spam.

## 7-day launch rhythm
- Day 1: Bot introduction + how it works.
- Day 2: Short video showing a real task completion.
- Day 3: Explain Normal vs VIP tasks.
- Day 4: Explain withdrawal rules and verification.
- Day 5: Referral explanation.
- Day 6: Real feature update / new task announcement.
- Day 7: Weekly recap + invite friends.
